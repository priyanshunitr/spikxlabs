## Process Section

### Section Heading

How the sprint works

### Step 1: Diagnose

We review your offer, audience, website, funnel, competitors, and current marketing assets to identify what is unclear, missing, or slowing conversion.

### Step 2: Shape

We define the strategy: positioning, message hierarchy, campaign angle, content structure, and the exact assets needed for the sprint.

### Step 3: Build

We design, write, and develop the agreed deliverables. This can include landing pages, creative assets, email copy, campaign structure, or brand messaging.

### Step 4: Launch

We prepare the final assets for use, connect the handoff pieces, and give your team a clear launch checklist.

### Step 5: Learn

We review performance signals and recommend next steps so the work can improve after launch instead of sitting still.

## FAQ Section

### Section Heading

Questions before we start

### FAQ 1: What kind of businesses do you work with?

We work with startups, creators, service businesses, consultants, education brands, and growing teams that need sharper marketing assets and clearer digital experiences.

### FAQ 2: Do you only design websites?

No. Websites are often part of the work, but we also help with positioning, campaign messaging, paid creative, email funnels, and launch strategy.

### FAQ 3: Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. We can work within your current brand system, improve what is already there, or create a cleaner direction if the current brand is not strong enough.

### FAQ 4: Do you handle development?

Yes. We can build responsive frontend pages and marketing websites. If a project needs complex backend functionality, we will define the scope clearly before starting.

### FAQ 5: How long does a sprint take?

Most focused sprints take 1 to 3 weeks depending on scope, feedback speed, and asset complexity.

### FAQ 6: What do you need from us before starting?

We usually need your offer details, target audience, existing website or assets, examples you like, business goals, and any current performance data.

### FAQ 7: Can you help with ads?

Yes. We can help with campaign angles, ad concepts, creative direction, landing pages, and testing plans. Media buying can be added if it fits the project scope.

### FAQ 8: Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Ongoing growth support is available for teams that need continuous campaign, website, creative, and funnel improvements.
